# Weight Loss Chat Processing Framework

Use this file after a Weight Loss rating chat has been completed and its abstraction decision has been approved.

This framework does not rate the task, select the abstraction frame, or supply prompts. Its job is to convert the completed chat and approved abstraction handoff into clear integration results for:

1. `weight_loss_manual_final.md`
2. `rating_comment_style_and_phrase_guide.md`

The governing contract is **integration result / no insertion**. Processing a chat does not create a presumption that either project document must change.

The manual stores reusable classification reasoning. The comment guide stores signed-off reusable wording. Keep those functions separate.

---

## 1. Required Inputs

Process a chat only when these inputs are available:

- the completed rating chat;
- the task screenshot, screenshots, or faithful OCR/transcription;
- the final rating and final comment;
- the user-approved abstraction handoff from the separate abstraction-selection step;
- the current Weight Loss manual;
- the current rating comment style and phrase guide;
- any research already performed in the rating chat, with sources or enough detail to verify it.

If an input is incomplete, identify the gap instead of filling it with assumptions.

---

## 2. Verdict Anchor and Conflict Rule

Treat the final rating comment from the completed chat as the **verdict anchor**. The processing task is normally to preserve and generalize the reasoning that supported that verdict, not to reopen the rating from scratch.

Do not treat the anchor as infallible. If the screenshot, faithful transcription, or verified research materially contradicts the final verdict or the approved abstraction handoff:

1. state the conflict precisely;
2. identify which proposed integration it blocks;
3. stop short of encoding the conflict as reusable guidance; and
4. request review before supplying a final insertion-ready block for the affected document.

A minor wording imperfection that does not alter the rating logic is not a material conflict.

---

## 3. Core Separation Rule

Every processed chat must return two separately labeled integration results, even if one or both recommend no insertion.

### Output A — Manual Integration Result

Target file: `weight_loss_manual_final.md`

Normal target section:

> Reusable Borderline Reasoning → Case-Derived Nodes

The result must be one of:

1. **New case-derived node** — the case contributes a genuinely new reusable reasoning pattern.
2. **Edit or extension to an existing node** — the case sharpens boundaries, adds a material variant, resolves an ambiguity, or supplies important negative direction without requiring a separate node.
3. **No insertion recommended** — existing official guidance, added guidance, an adapted seed node, or a case-derived node already covers the reusable reasoning sufficiently, or the case is too idiosyncratic to justify reusable material.

The manual result may contain:

- case type;
- final verdict anchor;
- visible evidence pattern;
- material research-resolved and unresolved findings;
- reusable inference;
- core reasoning path;
- decision shortcut;
- abandoned reasoning paths and negative direction;
- use and do-not-use boundaries;
- the relationship to existing guidance or nodes.

It must not become a polished comment template or general phrase bank.

### Output B — Comment-Guide Integration Result

Target file: `rating_comment_style_and_phrase_guide.md`

Normal target section:

> Signed-Off Weight Loss Comment Pattern Library

The result must be one of:

1. **New signed-off pattern or wording component** — the final comment contributes reusable wording with a sufficiently precise use condition.
2. **Edit or extension to an existing pattern** — a current pattern can absorb the useful wording, variant, hedge, clause, or warning.
3. **No insertion recommended** — the wording is already covered, is too case-specific, is not actually signed off, or adds no reusable value.

The guide result may contain:

- a precise use condition;
- an approved base template;
- optional sentence, clause, phrase-structure, or phrase variants;
- phrases to use carefully;
- a short boundary note needed for safe adaptation.

It must not reproduce the full classification analysis.

### Independence of the two results

The two results are independent. Valid outcomes include:

- insert into both documents;
- change the manual only;
- change the guide only;
- no insertion into either document.

Do not manufacture content merely to avoid a no-insertion result.

## 4. Weight Loss Evidence and Research Grounding

Begin with what the creative, page, or ad promotes. Do not substitute assumptions about what a viewer wants, what a product could be used for, or what might appear after a click.

Keep three evidence layers distinct throughout processing:

- **Visible task evidence** — text, imagery, layout, advertiser identity, product name, and other information present in the supplied task material.
- **Verified research** — externally confirmed identity, meaning, offer, campaign, provider, or purpose tied to something actually present in the task.
- **Inference** — the classification conclusion drawn from visible evidence and verified research.

Enumerate and research every visible identity or meaning candidate that could materially affect classification. This includes advertisers, brands, products, services, programs, procedures, slogans, acronyms, specialist terms, and ordinary-looking words or phrases with a plausible task-relevant meaning.

Search exact visible wording first. Use contextual variants when needed. Prefer authoritative primary sources and disambiguate same-named entities using the surrounding task context. Preserve material resolved, unresolved, ambiguous, and conflicting findings.

### Weight Loss pitch guardrail

Research may identify what a visible product, provider, program, campaign, or phrase is. It may establish a Weight Loss purpose when that purpose is verified for the exact advertised offer.

Research must not manufacture a missing Weight Loss pitch from:

- a brand's broad reputation;
- another product or campaign from the same company;
- a possible off-label or incidental use;
- a general association with health, fitness, dieting, or body image;
- unsupported assumptions about unseen landing-page content.

Ask whether the exact creative or exact research-resolved offer promotes weight loss or fat reduction as a particular purpose. Broad health relatedness is not enough by itself.

### Promoted claim, not medical truth

Classify the purpose or claim being promoted. Do not convert the processing step into an assessment of whether the advertised method is medically effective, safe, evidence-based, or likely to produce the claimed result unless the rating instructions expressly require that question.

---

## 5. Status of Adapted Seed Nodes

The manual's adapted seed nodes are translated starting hypotheses from the Video Games architecture. They are not completed Weight Loss case precedents.

When comparing a completed case with an adapted seed node:

- use the seed as an existing reusable structure;
- check whether the case validates it, materially refines it, exposes a boundary, or shows that it should be split or narrowed;
- do not call the seed “case-derived” merely because a new case resembles it;
- do not create a duplicate case-derived node when an edit or extension would integrate the reusable learning more cleanly;
- preserve the seed label unless the approved integration result explicitly justifies a status change.

Official guidance and added guidance remain controlling. Nodes organize application; they do not override the manual.

---

## 6. Using the Approved Abstraction Handoff

This framework does not select the abstraction frame. A separate pre-processing step proposes the abstraction, and the user supplies the approved handoff.

The handoff should identify:

- the manual-node frame;
- the decisive node-level conditions;
- supporting evidence;
- anchor-only details;
- the causal-condition removal result;
- the point at which further abstraction would become too broad;
- the reusable decision principle;
- the relationship to existing guidance and nodes;
- the proposed manual integration direction;
- the reusable comment wording scope;
- the relationship to existing guide patterns;
- the proposed guide integration direction;
- the evidence and research basis;
- material unresolved findings.

Use the approved handoff as controlling input. Do not silently broaden, narrow, replace, or reselect its abstraction.

Review the current documents to implement the handoff accurately and avoid duplication. That review does not authorize a different abstraction frame.

If the handoff is ambiguous about a decision that would materially change the resulting insertion, identify the ambiguity for review. Do not guess.

If the source material or verified research conflicts materially with the handoff, apply the conflict rule in Section 2.

---

## 7. Node Matching and Integration Logic

Compare the approved abstraction with all materially applicable official guidance, added guidance, adapted seed nodes, and case-derived nodes.

Rank applicable nodes by fit:

- **Direct** — captures the decisive evidence relationship and category boundary.
- **Partial** — captures a material part of the reasoning but requires another component.
- **Broad** — supplies useful background or a general boundary without resolving the case alone.

Assign each applicable node a role:

- **Primary** — carries the central reusable reasoning.
- **Supporting** — supplies a material secondary component.
- **Contrast** — clarifies why a nearby interpretation or outcome does not apply.

Nodes are modular, not votes. Several can apply at once, and a lower-ranked node may supply the best boundary or negative direction for one part of the case.

### Causal-condition removal test

For every proposed eligibility condition, ask:

> If this feature were removed while the same reasoning still applied, should the case remain eligible for the node or pattern?

If yes, the feature is supporting evidence or anchor detail rather than a defining condition. Do not make incidental case details part of the reusable rule.

### Choosing the manual integration result

Recommend a new node only when the reusable causal structure is not already represented clearly enough.

Prefer an edit or extension when the case:

- sharpens an existing boundary;
- adds a material variant under the same causal structure;
- contributes a useful decision shortcut or abandoned path;
- validates and improves an adapted seed without requiring a new abstraction.

Recommend no insertion when:

- current guidance already handles the case without meaningful loss;
- the proposed difference is only a new product, advertiser, visual treatment, or other anchor detail;
- the abstraction would be so broad that it merely restates official guidance;
- the abstraction would be so narrow that it would not transfer;
- evidence is too uncertain to support a reusable rule;
- the approved handoff itself calls for no manual insertion.

## 8. Manual Node Format

For a new case-derived node, use this structure:

```md
## Case-Derived Node: [Short reusable title]

### Source / status
Case-derived from a completed and approved Weight Loss rating chat.

### Case type
[Recurring borderline pattern stated without incidental anchor details.]

### Final verdict anchor
Rating: Yes / No / Unrateable

Final comment anchor:
> [Final signed-off comment or a clearly labeled concise paraphrase.]

### Evidence / research pattern
- Visible: [...]
- Research-resolved: [...]
- Material unresolved or conflicting finding: [None / ...]

### Core reasoning path
[Reusable path from evidence through verified identity or purpose to the verdict.]

### Decision shortcut
[Compact rule taught by the case.]

### Abandoned reasoning paths / negative direction
- Do not [...]
- Avoid [...]
- [Rejected interpretation] was rejected because [...]

### Use this node when
- [...]

### Do not use this node when
- [...]
```

For an edit or extension, quote the existing node title, identify the exact insertion location, and provide only the replacement or additional text needed.

For no insertion, identify the controlling existing guidance or node and explain briefly why it already covers the reusable reasoning.

---

## 9. Comment-Guide Pattern Format

For a new signed-off wording pattern or component, use this structure:

```md
### [Pattern number and reusable title]

Use when [precise condition based on task evidence, including any materially relevant verified research].

Approved base template:
> [Signed-off reusable wording.]

Optional variants:
> [Variant.]

Phrases to use carefully:
- “[phrase]” — [why it needs care]

Notes:
- [Adaptation or boundary note.]
```

Reusable value may exist at any level: complete template, sentence, clause, contrast structure, hedge, or individual phrase. Extract only what the approved wording scope authorizes.

Review the signed-off Weight Loss library before proposing a new pattern. The optional cross-category wording bank may inspire phrasing, but it is not precedent and does not make wording signed off.

Prefer an edit or extension when an existing signed-off pattern can absorb the useful wording naturally. Recommend no insertion when the final comment contributes nothing distinct or safely reusable.

Do not create a separate abstract comment “principle” merely to parallel a manual node.

---

## 10. Processing Workflow

1. Confirm that all required inputs are present.
2. Identify the final rating and final comment, and record them as the verdict anchor.
3. Extract the visible evidence without merging it with research or inference.
4. Enumerate and research every materially plausible visible identity or meaning candidate.
5. Preserve material resolved, unresolved, ambiguous, and conflicting research findings.
6. Apply the Weight Loss pitch guardrail: tie researched purpose to the exact visible or research-resolved offer.
7. Compare the evidence and research with the verdict anchor and approved handoff. Flag any material conflict before drafting insertion-ready content.
8. Use the approved abstraction handoff as the controlling frame; do not perform a new abstraction selection.
9. Compare that frame with all materially applicable guidance and nodes, and record their fit and role.
10. Apply the causal-condition removal test to proposed reusable conditions.
11. Choose Output A: new node, edit/extension, or no insertion.
12. Extract abandoned interpretations, negative direction, and use boundaries authorized by the handoff.
13. Review the signed-off comment pattern library and the approved wording scope.
14. Choose Output B: new wording, edit/extension, or no insertion.
15. Draft both integration results using the contract in Section 11.
16. Run the quality checks in Section 12.

---

## 11. Required Integration-Result Contract

Return the result in this order.

### A. Manual integration result

- **Result:** New case-derived node / Edit or extension / No insertion recommended
- **Target:** Exact file, section, and existing node if applicable
- **Basis:** Concise explanation tied to the approved handoff and current manual
- **Applicable guidance/nodes:** Ranked by fit and labeled Primary, Supporting, or Contrast
- **Insertion-ready material:** Exact block or exact edit; write `None` for no insertion
- **Duplication/boundary note:** Why this result does not duplicate or distort existing guidance
- **Conflict flag:** None, or the precise conflict requiring review

### B. Comment-guide integration result

- **Result:** New signed-off pattern/component / Edit or extension / No insertion recommended
- **Target:** Exact file, section, and existing pattern if applicable
- **Basis:** Concise explanation tied to the approved wording scope and current guide
- **Insertion-ready material:** Exact block or exact edit; write `None` for no insertion
- **Adaptation note:** What may be changed safely in future comments
- **Conflict flag:** None, or the precise conflict requiring review

### C. Processing record

- **Verdict anchor:** Rating and final comment
- **Visible evidence used:** [...]
- **Verified research used:** [...]
- **Material unresolved findings:** None / [...]
- **Inference preserved:** [...]
- **Approved abstraction handoff followed:** Yes / No — explain any deviation or blocker

Do not replace a no-insertion recommendation with a speculative draft. `None` is the correct insertion-ready material when no document change is justified.

---

## 12. Quality Checks

Before finalizing, confirm:

- Did I return both integration results, including an explicit no-insertion result where appropriate?
- Did I keep manual reasoning separate from reusable comment wording?
- Did I preserve the final comment as the verdict anchor?
- Did I flag rather than encode any material conflict with the anchor or approved handoff?
- Did I separate visible evidence, verified research, and inference?
- Did I research every visible identity or meaning candidate that could materially affect classification?
- Did I preserve material unresolved, ambiguous, or conflicting findings?
- Did I tie researched purpose to the exact offer instead of importing a general brand association?
- Did I classify the promoted Weight Loss claim rather than its medical effectiveness?
- Did I distinguish broad health relatedness from particular Weight Loss or fat-reduction purpose?
- Did I use the approved abstraction handoff without independently reframing it?
- Did I compare all materially applicable guidance and nodes and assign fit and role?
- Did I apply the causal-condition removal test?
- Did I treat adapted seed nodes as seeds rather than completed case precedents?
- Did I prefer an edit or no insertion when a new node would duplicate existing material?
- Did I preserve abandoned reasoning paths and negative direction where useful?
- Did I review existing signed-off comment patterns before proposing new wording?
- Did I keep optional cross-category wording in its non-authoritative role?
- Is every proposed use condition transferable rather than tied to an incidental advertiser or visual detail?
- Is all insertion-ready material placed under the correct project section?

---

## 13. Scope Boundary

This framework begins only after the rating chat is complete and the abstraction handoff is approved.

The first real Weight Loss case application belongs in that case's respective chat. This document therefore contains no fabricated worked case, no initial signed-off case precedent, and no embedded operational prompts.
